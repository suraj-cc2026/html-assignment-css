Good work starts with good questions.

I’m an independent designer based in Portland, Oregon. I partner with kind people and purpose-led teams to shape identities, digital experiences, and stories that feel both useful and human.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me tending to too many houseplants, browsing a bookstore, or taking the scenic route home. I believe the best creative work comes from paying attention—and staying open to what’s next.
